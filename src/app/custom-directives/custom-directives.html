• What is Custom Directives in Angular?
    • Customer Directives in Angular allow you to extend the capabilities of HTML by creating a reusable behavious, styling or structure logic,
      that can be directly applied to the DOM elements.
  • What are different type of custom directives in Angular?
    • Angular offer primarily 2 custom directives :
      • Attribute Directive:
        • It changes the appreance or behaviou of an element.
      • Structural Directives:
        • It modify the DOM by adding or removing elements
